About the site

Ward volunteers maintain this website for local announcements, scheduling, and coordination. Confirm meeting times and official information through Church channels or a ward leader. Use Church-provided systems for official Church records.

Using the site

  • Use your own contact details and submit accurate information. Get permission before sharing someone else’s information or photos.
  • Keep confidential Church information, passwords, and sensitive personal details out of submissions, uploads, and sync runs.
  • Access Leaders only if authorized. Protect your sign-in and use only the records you are authorized to access.
  • Keep submissions respectful and relevant. Do not use the site for commercial advertising, harassment, spam, or unauthorized collection of information. Administrators may remove submissions or restrict access when needed.

Availability and other services

Features, listings, and schedules may change. Site availability and the accuracy of posted information are not guaranteed. Other websites and services have their own terms and privacy policies.

Questions

Contact the ward with questions about the site or these terms. The website Privacy Policy describes how the site handles information.

This independently maintained website is not an official ward website or an official website of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. It is not sponsored or endorsed by the Church.
