Who runs the site

Ward volunteers maintain this website. This policy describes how the website handles information.

Information the site handles

  • Forms and sign-ups. The site receives information you enter, which can include names, contact details, dates, availability, messages, and attachments. Volunteers use it to coordinate meals, appointments, service listings, and ward activities and to respond to requests.
  • Attendance and Leaders tools. The site can process member and guest check-ins, leader sign-in information, and planning records. Ward Sync can copy selected reports from Leader and Clerk Resources (LCR) to Leaders and send reviewed attendance to LCR. Keep confidential Church information in Church-provided systems.
  • Browser and connection data. If you choose to remember a check-in name, it is saved in this browser on this device. Use Forget saved names on the check-in page to remove those names from the device. The site can also store session information, settings, and listing-management keys in your browser. Hosting and database providers may process technical details such as your IP address and request logs to deliver and secure the site. Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts.

Who can see information

Authorized leaders can access information needed to manage the site and respond to requests. Announcements, published programs, approved listings, and information displayed on sign-up pages may be visible to visitors. Only include details you intend the relevant audience to see. The site uses its hosting provider and Supabase to deliver the site and store or process information.

Retention and requests

Information may remain while it is needed for scheduling, coordination, or follow-up. Contact the ward to ask about information held by this site or request a correction or removal. You can clear browser storage in your browser settings. For official Church records, use the Church’s privacy channels.
